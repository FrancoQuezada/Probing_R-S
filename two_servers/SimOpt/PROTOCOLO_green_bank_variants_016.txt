I0252: historical-bank Green variants (fixed before full execution)

Execution path: 016 -> 012 -> 011 -> 010 -> probing_regime_model -> 003.
Conceptual predecessor: ../2serversModel/probing_three_searches_crn_oracle_surface.jl
(5000 paths, 45 updates, other alpha, beta and model). It is NOT I0252.
I0252 settings copied exactly from 011: z0=(.12,.10), N=500, 45 jobs,
144 policies, 100 updates, eta=.035/sqrt(k), componentwise gradient clip +-15,
projection [0,1]^2. No early stopping. Only reps 1,2,3 are requested.
Objective J(z)=max_i E_z[Y_i]-alpha'z, one common ex-ante routing policy.
Not E[max_i Y_i], not the SimOpt009 objective, not a new two-stage training model.

Methods: original Green; windows 5,10,20 including current; exponential
rho=.7,.9,.95 over all banks, bank weights a proportional to rho^(t-h);
filters kappa=1,2,5. Same bank weights for all policy values and the selected
policy's LR-score gradient. Probing cost and its derivative subtracted once.
Uniform retained-bank estimators delegate to original R.green_data, preserving
its original floating-point summation order. rho=1 delegates identically.
No self-normalized importance sampling. ESS normalization is diagnostic only.
Likelihoods and scores evaluated at target z and each actual bank's generating z.
Original probability clamp [1e-12,1-1e-12], log LR clamp [-35,35] unchanged.
LR clipping can bias values/gradients. Existing derivative at probability clamp
boundaries is preserved. Clipping counts reported, no observation-level filtering.

Filter: original full Green provisional policy; corrected sample variance of
Y_provisional*clipped_LR in EACH bank at CURRENT target z; retain V_h <= kappa*V_current.
The non-strict inequality resolves zero-variance ties: if V_current=0 retain
zero-variance banks plus current; always retain current regardless of threshold.
Invalid or nonfinite LR, score, reward, variance or threshold raises explicitly;
no fabricated replacement and no individual observation removal. Recompute one
common best policy and gradient on retained banks; it may differ from provisional.
Selection is adaptive to the observed rewards and can bias the estimator.

CRN: unchanged X.seed(I0252,compare,rep,search,k), independent of method.
Every method generates 500 new paths each update, totaling 50000 per search,
1500000 across 30 full searches. Historical processing generates NO paths.
Rolling implementation retains discarded banks in memory for accounting only;
they make no contribution to the estimator. Retained paths and historical reuse
exclude neither observations nor parts of a bank. Reuse excludes the current bank.

Diagnostics use the ACTUAL final selected policy, with filter variances and
provisional policy stored separately. bank CSV has weights, rejection identities,
sample variances of Y*LR and Y*LR*score_j. iteration variance is the weighted
average of within-bank sample variances (NOT variance of LR alone). The additional
sum_h a_h^2*s_h^2/n_h is a descriptive mean-variance proxy, not a valid adaptive
optimization uncertainty estimate. ESS uses observation coefficients a_h*L_hj/n_h:
ESS=(sum c)^2/sum c^2. Raw unweighted pooled LR ESS stored separately. Neither
is the retained observation count or a guarantee of gradient precision.

Times: searches sequential, BLAS=1, warmup tests excluded. Primary optimization
clock includes generation, materialization, 144-policy rewards, original local
data work, bank selection/weighting (including ALL filter variance calculations),
Green estimator, original projected step and core diagnostics. Additional moment
diagnostics, CSV/checkpoint I/O and external evaluations excluded and timed
separately. Diagnostic allocation/GC can influence subsequent search times;
OS not isolated; comparisons descriptive, not controlled performance benchmarks.

Final independent evaluation: original per-rep selector seed, 10000 direct paths;
select one policy for each endpoint. Original independent holdout seed, 50000
direct paths for THAT fixed selected policy; same primitives across endpoints.
No LR. Conditional MC SE=s/sqrt(50000), normal 95% CI, no policy reselection on
holdout. It evaluates the policy selected using the independent selector, which
can fall short of max_i E[Y_i]. Paired endpoint differences use holdout CRN.
Trace plots: independent fixed 1000-path CRN bank, direct max of policy means;
selection bias remains in trace curves and conditional SE is not post-selection
inference. Never substitute those traces for final holdout estimates.
Across-rep SD measures variation across three optimization/evaluation replications;
it differs from conditional within-holdout MC SE. Three seeds are exploratory.
Surface is the archived 21x21, 5000-path direct CRN surface; no recomputation,
no certification of a global optimum. All figures PNG and PDF.

Small tests before full runs: all-bank limits exact, current LR exactly 1,
normalized nonnegative weights, same policy in value and gradient, current kept,
zero variance ties, invalid inputs explicit errors, fixed-seed search reproducible.
